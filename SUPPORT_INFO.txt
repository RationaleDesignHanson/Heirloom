FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
================================================================================

GETTING STARTED
----------------

Q: Do I need to create an account?
A: No! Heirloom doesn't require account creation. Just download and start adding recipes.

Q: How do I add my first recipe?
A: Tap the + button and choose:
   - "Import from URL" to import from a website
   - "Add Manually" to type in your own recipe
   - "From Photo" to scan a cookbook page (coming soon)

Q: Is there a limit to how many recipes I can save?
A: No limit! Save as many recipes as you want. Storage is limited only by your device and iCloud space.

RECIPE IMPORT
-------------

Q: Which websites can I import recipes from?
A: Heirloom works with any site that uses Schema.org recipe markup, including:
   - AllRecipes.com
   - Serious Eats
   - King Arthur Baking
   - Most major food blogs

   If a site doesn't work, email us and we'll try to add support!

Q: Why didn't my recipe import correctly?
A: Some sites don't structure their recipes in a way we can read. Try:
   - Checking if the URL copied correctly (no extra spaces)
   - Making sure you're copying the recipe page URL (not homepage)
   - Manually adding the recipe if import fails

Q: Can I edit imported recipes?
A: Yes! Tap the recipe, then tap "Edit" to modify any part of it.

ICLOUD SYNC
-----------

Q: How do I enable iCloud sync?
A: iCloud sync is automatic if you're signed into iCloud. Check:
   iOS Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Heirloom → Enable

Q: My recipes aren't syncing between devices. Why?
A: Check these:
   - You're signed into the same Apple ID on both devices
   - iCloud sync is enabled for Heirloom on both devices
   - You have available iCloud storage
   - Both devices have internet connection
   - Give it a few minutes - initial sync can take time

Q: Can I use Heirloom without iCloud?
A: Yes! Recipes are always stored locally on your device. iCloud sync is optional.

Q: How much iCloud storage does Heirloom use?
A: Very little! A typical recipe with a photo uses about 200-500KB. Even 1000 recipes would only use ~200-500MB.

SHOPPING LIST
-------------

Q: How do I add recipes to my shopping list?
A: Open a recipe, tap the shopping cart icon, then "Add to Shopping List"

Q: Why are ingredients combined in my shopping list?
A: Heirloom automatically combines identical ingredients across recipes.
   For example: "2 cups flour" + "3 cups flour" = "5 cups flour total"

Q: Can I export my shopping list?
A: Yes! Open Shopping List, tap "Export to Reminders". This creates a
   "Heirloom Shopping" list in the iOS Reminders app.

Q: Do I need to grant Reminders permission?
A: Only if you want to export shopping lists. Tap "Export to Reminders"
   and iOS will ask for permission. You can revoke it anytime in
   iOS Settings → Heirloom → Reminders.

COOKING MODE
------------

Q: What is Cooking Mode?
A: A distraction-free, step-by-step cooking experience with large text
   that's easy to read while cooking.

Q: How do I use Cooking Mode?
A: Open any recipe and tap "Start Cooking" at the bottom.

Q: Does Cooking Mode track my progress?
A: Yes! Mark steps as complete, and when you finish, Heirloom increments
   the "times cooked" counter.

RECIPE SCALING
--------------

Q: How do I scale a recipe?
A: Open the recipe and use the +/- buttons next to "Servings" to adjust
   from 0.5x to 10x. Ingredient quantities update automatically.

Q: Why don't all my ingredients scale?
A: Heirloom only scales ingredients with recognized quantities.
   Ingredients like "salt to taste" or "1 package yeast" don't scale
   automatically.

SEARCH AND FILTERS
------------------

Q: How do I search for recipes?
A: Use the search bar at the top of the recipe list. Search by recipe name.

Q: What filters are available?
A: Tap the filter icon to filter by:
   - Source (Family, URL, Manual, Cookbook)
   - Favorites only
   - In shopping list
   - Cooked / Never cooked
   - Sort by date, title, or times cooked

DATA AND PRIVACY
----------------

Q: Where is my recipe data stored?
A: Locally on your device and (if enabled) in your personal iCloud account.

Q: Can you see my recipes?
A: No! We have zero access to your recipe data. It's private to you.

Q: What data do you collect?
A: We collect anonymous usage analytics (like "user opened cooking mode")
   to improve the app. We do NOT collect recipe content, personal info,
   or anything that identifies you. See Privacy Policy for details.

Q: How do I delete all my data?
A: Settings → "Clear All Data" → Confirm. This deletes all recipes from
   your device. To delete from iCloud: iOS Settings → iCloud → Manage
   Storage → Heirloom → Delete Data.

TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------

Q: The app is crashing. What should I do?
A: Try:
   1. Force quit and reopen the app
   2. Restart your device
   3. Check for app updates in the App Store
   4. If still crashing, email us with details

Q: Recipe images aren't loading.
A: Try:
   1. Check your internet connection
   2. Make sure you have available device storage
   3. Try deleting and re-importing the recipe
   4. Contact us if the problem persists

Q: I found a bug!
A: Please report it! Use Settings → Send Feedback or email us with:
   - What you were doing when the bug occurred
   - What you expected to happen
   - What actually happened
   - Screenshots if possible

Q: The app is running slowly.
A: Try:
   1. Close other apps running in the background
   2. Restart your device
   3. Check for iOS updates
   4. Ensure you have at least 1GB of free storage
