================================================================================
GETTING STARTED
================================================================================

1. INSTALL TESTFLIGHT
   - Download TestFlight from the App Store (if not already installed)
   - It's Apple's official beta testing app

2. ACCEPT THE INVITATION
   - Check your email for the Heirloom TestFlight invitation
   - Click "View in TestFlight" or copy the link
   - Open in TestFlight app and tap "Accept"

3. INSTALL HEIRLOOM
   - Tap "Install" in TestFlight
   - Wait for download to complete
   - Launch Heirloom from your home screen

4. COMPLETE ONBOARDING
   - Follow the 4-page onboarding flow
   - Grant permissions when requested:
     * iCloud (for syncing recipes across devices)
     * Notifications (for cooking timers - optional)
     * Reminders (for shopping list export - optional)

HOW TO REPORT BUGS
================================================================================

We need your help finding issues! When you encounter a bug:

OPTION 1: TestFlight Feedback (Preferred)
-----------------------------------------
1. Open TestFlight app
2. Select Heirloom
3. Tap "Send Beta Feedback"
4. Include screenshot if possible
5. Describe what happened

OPTION 2: Email
--------------
Send to: [YOUR EMAIL]
Subject: "Heirloom Beta Bug Report"

Please include:
• Device model (e.g., iPhone 14 Pro)
• iOS version (e.g., iOS 17.5)
• Steps to reproduce
• What you expected vs. what happened
• Screenshots or screen recording (if helpful)
• App version/build (found in Settings tab)

BUG PRIORITY GUIDE:
• CRITICAL: App crashes, data loss, can't use core features
• HIGH: Feature broken or major usability issue
• MEDIUM: Minor bug with workaround available
• LOW: Cosmetic issue or small annoyance

TIPS FOR SUCCESSFUL TESTING
================================================================================

1. USE IT LIKE YOU WOULD A REAL APP
   - Import your actual recipes
   - Use it for real cooking sessions
   - Build your actual shopping list
   - This gives the most valuable feedback

2. TRY TO BREAK IT
   - Import weird recipe formats
   - Scale recipes to extreme values (0.25x, 10x)
   - Add 50+ recipes to test performance
   - Go offline and online repeatedly

3. TEST EDGE CASES
   - Very long recipe titles (50+ characters)
   - Recipes with no images
   - Unusual ingredient formats
   - Recipes with 20+ ingredients or 15+ steps

4. PROVIDE CONTEXT
   - "Export to Reminders failed" = okay
   - "Export to Reminders failed when I tapped Export on shopping list
      with 15 items from 3 recipes. iPhone 14 Pro, iOS 17.5." = great!

5. BE HONEST
   - We want real feedback, not just positivity
   - If something is confusing or broken, tell us
   - Your honest input makes the app better

FAQ
================================================================================

Q: Will my recipes sync across my iPhone and iPad?
A: Yes, if you're signed into iCloud on both devices. Sync happens
   automatically in the background.

Q: Can I use Heirloom without iCloud?
A: Yes! Heirloom works fine without iCloud. You'll just lose cross-device
   sync and recipe sharing features.

Q: What happens to my recipes after beta ends?
A: Your recipes stay in your iCloud account. When the public app launches,
   you'll seamlessly transition with all your data intact.

Q: Can I share recipes with non-beta testers?
A: Recipe sharing only works between Heirloom users (beta or public).
   For now, share with other beta testers only.

Q: How do I find my app version/build number?
A: Open Heirloom → Settings tab → scroll to bottom → see "Version 1.0.0 (1)"

Q: The app crashed! What should I do?
A: Apple automatically sends crash reports via TestFlight. Please also email
   us with what you were doing when it crashed so we can reproduce it.

Q: I want to suggest a new feature. Where do I send it?
A: Email [YOUR EMAIL] or include in the Week 1 survey. We're collecting all
   feature requests to prioritize for future updates.

Q: Can I invite friends to the beta?
A: Not yet - we're keeping this initial beta small. If you know someone who
   would be a great tester, send their email to [YOUR EMAIL] and we'll
   consider expanding the group.
