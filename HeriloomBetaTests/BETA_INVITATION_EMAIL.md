**Subject:** Did you know Heirloom can do this? (Beta Week 2)

Hi [First Name],

Quick email to highlight a few Heirloom features you might not have discovered yet:

### 🎉 Feature Spotlight

**1. Smart Recipe Scaling**
Tap the serving size in any recipe to scale it up or down. Heirloom intelligently adjusts spices, leavening agents, and eggs (not just simple multiplication).

**2. Dinner Party Planning**
Tap the + button → Dinner Party. Add multiple recipes and Heirloom calculates exactly when to start each dish so everything finishes together.

**3. Shopping List Aggregation**
Add 3-4 recipes to your shopping list. Heirloom automatically combines duplicates (e.g., "2 cups flour" + "1 cup flour" = "3 cups flour").

**4. Recipe Sharing**
Tap the share button on any recipe to send it to another Heirloom user via CloudKit. Try sharing with [another beta tester's name] if you know them!

**5. Cooking Mode**
Tap "Start Cooking" for full-screen, step-by-step guidance. Auto-tracks "times cooked" when you finish.
