HEIRLOOM PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: December 19, 2024

================================================================================
INTRODUCTION
================================================================================

Heirloom ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our iOS application.

By using Heirloom, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

================================================================================
INFORMATION WE COLLECT
================================================================================

1. INFORMATION YOU PROVIDE

Recipe Data:
- Recipe titles, ingredients, instructions, notes
- Recipe photos you add or import
- Cooking times, serving sizes, and other recipe metadata
- Source information (URLs, cookbook references, family attribution)
- Your personal ratings, favorites, and cooking history

Social Features Data (When You Choose to Share):
- Recipes you explicitly choose to share with others
- Comments you add to recipes and their visibility settings
- Recipe lineage information (who shared the recipe, generation tracking)
- Personal messages attached to shared recipes
- Comment endorsements and upvotes

User Preferences:
- App settings and preferences
- Shopping list selections
- Filter and sort preferences
- Privacy and sharing consent choices

2. AUTOMATICALLY COLLECTED INFORMATION

Usage Analytics (via Mixpanel):
- Feature usage patterns (which features you use and how often)
- App performance data (load times, errors, crashes)
- Device information (iOS version, device model, screen size)
- Session data (app opens, duration, screens viewed)
- Recipe import success/failure rates
- Anonymous interaction events (button taps, navigation patterns)

Technical Data:
- App version and build number
- Device identifier (anonymized)
- Operating system version
- Network type (WiFi, cellular)

3. INFORMATION WE DO NOT COLLECT

We do NOT collect:
- Your name, email address, or contact information
- Location data or GPS coordinates
- Photos from your photo library (except those you explicitly select for recipes)
- Payment or financial information
- Health or biometric data
- Personal identification numbers
- Contacts or address book data
- Browsing history outside the app

================================================================================
HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
================================================================================

We use your information for the following purposes:

1. To Provide and Maintain the App:
- Store and sync your recipes across your devices via iCloud
- Display your recipes, shopping lists, and cooking history
- Import recipes from URLs you provide
- Export shopping lists to iOS Reminders (with your permission)

2. To Improve the App:
- Analyze usage patterns to understand which features are most valuable
- Identify and fix bugs, crashes, and performance issues
- Optimize user experience based on aggregate behavior
- Plan future features based on how you use the app

3. To Communicate with You:
- Respond to your support requests or feedback
- Send important updates about the app (via App Store only)
- Notify you of privacy policy changes

================================================================================
HOW WE STORE YOUR INFORMATION
================================================================================

Recipe Data Storage:
- Your recipe data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData
- Recipe images are stored in your device's local file system
- All recipe data is private to your device

iCloud Sync:
- If enabled, your recipe data syncs via your personal iCloud account
- iCloud sync uses Apple's CloudKit service
- Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest using Apple's security
- We do not have access to your iCloud data
- Your data is subject to Apple's iCloud Privacy Policy

Recipe Sharing (CloudKit Public Database):
- When you explicitly share a recipe, it's stored in Apple's CloudKit public database
- Shared recipes include: title, ingredients, instructions, photos, and optional messages
- Recipients can import shared recipes into their own collections
- Shared comments (when you choose "Share with Family") are visible to anyone with that recipe
- Lineage tracking connects shared recipe copies to track family history
- You control what you share - nothing is shared without your explicit action
- Shared data is encrypted in transit and stored on Apple's secure servers
- You can choose privacy levels for comments: Private, Share with Family, or Public

Comment Visibility:
- Private comments: Only visible to you on your device
- Share with Family comments: Visible to anyone who has your shared recipe
- Public comments: Visible to the broader Heirloom community (future feature)
- You choose the visibility level for each comment

Analytics Data:
- Anonymous usage data is stored on Mixpanel's secure servers
- Data is aggregated and cannot be traced back to you personally
- No recipe content or personal information is sent to analytics

================================================================================
RECIPE SHARING AND SOCIAL FEATURES
================================================================================

Heirloom includes optional social features to share recipes with family and friends.
These features are OFF by default and require your explicit consent.

What Gets Shared:
When you explicitly choose to share a recipe, the following data is shared via Apple's CloudKit:
- Recipe title, ingredients, instructions, and metadata
- Recipe photos (if you choose to include them)
- Optional personal message you write
- Generation tracking (who shared it to you, how many times it's been shared)
- Comments you mark as "Share with Family" or "Public"
- Recipe lineage information (provenance tracking)

What Does NOT Get Shared:
- Private comments (marked as "Private")
- Personal notes unless you explicitly include them
- Your ratings or cooking history
- Shopping lists
- Other recipes in your collection
- Any data you haven't explicitly chosen to share

Share Links:
- Shared recipes generate a unique share link
- Share links can be sent via Messages, Email, AirDrop, or QR code
- Recipients can preview and import the shared recipe
- Share links don't expire but can be deleted by you
- Anyone with the share link can view and import the recipe

Comment Sharing Across Recipe Lineage:
- When you share a recipe and add "Share with Family" comments, those comments follow the recipe
- Recipients who import your recipe can see your shared comments
- If they re-share the recipe, your comments continue to follow it
- You can endorse helpful comments from others in your recipe lineage
- All participants in a recipe lineage can see shared comments
- Private comments are never shared

Consent and Control:
- Sharing features require your consent on first use
- You can enable/disable sharing in Settings → Privacy
- You can delete previously shared recipes from CloudKit
- You can change comment visibility at any time
- Withdrawing consent doesn't affect recipes already shared (they remain accessible)

Third-Party Services:

- Purpose: Import recipes from URLs you provide

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., court orders, subpoenas).

YOUR PRIVACY RIGHTS AND CHOICES
================================================================================

You have the following rights regarding your data:

1. Access and Portability:
- All your recipe data is stored locally on your device
- You can view all your data within the app at any time
- Export recipes manually by sharing them

2. Deletion:
- Delete individual recipes at any time within the app
- Delete all data via Settings → "Clear All Data"
- Uninstalling the app deletes all local data
- To delete iCloud data: iOS Settings → iCloud → Manage Storage → Heirloom

3. Control iCloud Sync:
- Enable/disable in iOS Settings → iCloud → Heirloom
- When disabled, recipes remain local to each device

4. Control Recipe Sharing:
- Enable/disable recipe sharing in Settings → Privacy → Recipe Sharing
- Choose whether to require consent before sharing recipes
- View and manage previously shared recipes
- Export your shared recipe data
- Delete shared recipes from CloudKit

5. Control Comment Sharing:
- Choose default comment visibility (Private/Family/Public)
- Change visibility for individual comments at any time
- View comments you've shared across all recipes
- Opt-out of comment aggregation

6. Control Analytics:
- Enable/disable anonymous usage analytics in Settings → Privacy → Analytics
- Analytics data is anonymous and cannot identify you
- Opt-out at any time without affecting app functionality

7. Reminders Permission:
- Grant or revoke in iOS Settings → Heirloom → Reminders
- Only used when you explicitly export shopping lists

8. Photo Library Permission:
- Grant or revoke in iOS Settings → Heirloom → Photos
- Only accessed when you add photos to recipes

9. Two-Tier Consent Model:
- Consent 1: Recipe Sharing Features (share recipes, view shared recipes, pass-down)
- Consent 2: Anonymous Usage Analytics (help improve the app)
- Both are opt-in and can be changed at any time
- You can use the app fully without enabling either consent option
