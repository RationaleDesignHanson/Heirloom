```

↓                              ↓                              ↓
```

## Revenue Model

### Pricing Strategy

**Free Tier:**
- 100 recipes max
- Basic search
- Manual recipe entry
- Ad-supported (light)

**Premium Tier:**
- **$4.99/month** or **$39.99/year** (save $20)
- Unlimited recipes
- AI-powered features (extraction, meal planning)
- Family sharing (up to 5 people)
- No ads
- Priority support

**Why this pricing?**
- Competitors: Paprika ($4.99 one-time), Mela ($4.99/month), AnyList ($9.99/year)
- Our positioning: Premium AI features justify subscription
- Annual discount encourages commitment

### Conversion Strategy

**Free-to-Premium Funnel:**
1. **100-recipe limit** (soft paywall)
2. **In-app prompts** after key actions ("Want unlimited recipes?")
3. **7-day free trial** (reduce friction)
4. **Feature teasers** (show locked features to free users)

**Target Conversion Rate:** 12%
- Conservative: 8%
- Baseline: 12%
- Optimistic: 15%

## Risk Mitigation

### What Could Go Wrong?

**Risk 1: Product Hunt launch flops**
- Mitigation: Prepare backup channels (Reddit, Twitter)
- Fallback: Double down on press outreach

**Risk 2: Conversion rate <8%**
- Mitigation: A/B test pricing ($2.99, $4.99, $6.99)
- Fallback: Adjust premium features or free tier limits

**Risk 3: Tech press ignores us**
- Mitigation: Influencer marketing can compensate
- Fallback: Community-driven growth (Reddit, Facebook)

**Risk 4: Paid ads don't work**
- Mitigation: Start small, test, iterate
- Fallback: Skip paid ads, focus on organic

**Risk 5: Competitors launch similar features**
- Mitigation: Speed is our advantage (launch Q1)
- Fallback: Differentiate on AI quality and UX

## Go/No-Go Gates

### Decision Points

**After Closed Beta (Jan 15):**
- [ ] <5% crash rate? → If no, delay 1 week
- [ ] 4.5+ internal rating? → If no, fix critical issues
- [ ] All P0/P1 bugs fixed? → If no, don't expand beta

**After Expanded Beta (Feb 1):**
- [ ] <2% crash rate? → If no, delay soft launch
- [ ] 60%+ Day 7 retention? → If no, improve onboarding
- [ ] 4.5+ internal rating? → If no, polish more

**After Soft Launch (Feb 15):**
- [ ] 500+ downloads? → If no, delay full launch
- [ ] 4.0+ App Store rating? → If no, fix UX issues
- [ ] 8%+ conversion? → If no, revise pricing/features

**If any gate fails:** Pause, fix, retest. Don't rush to launch.

## Questions?

**Q: Why not launch big right away?**
A: We need to validate product-market fit first. Beta → Soft Launch → Full Launch de-risks the investment.

**Q: What if we don't hit 15K downloads?**
A: Success is relative. Even 10K downloads at 12% conversion = $36K ARR, which validates the model.

**Q: Why subscription vs. one-time purchase?**
A: AI costs scale with usage. Subscription funds ongoing development + AI infrastructure.

**Q: When do we break even?**
A: Baseline scenario: Month 12. Optimistic: Month 8. Depends on conversion rate and costs.

**Q: What happens after Q1?**
A: Focus shifts from acquisition to retention and feature expansion. Explore fundraising if growth is strong.
