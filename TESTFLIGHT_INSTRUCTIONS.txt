Heirloom TestFlight - Phase 2A Testing

Welcome to Heirloom TestFlight! This build focuses on testing CloudKit recipe sharing between devices.

WHAT'S NEW
- CloudKit sharing: Share recipes with friends and family
- Generation tracking: See who shared recipes and when
- Provenance metadata: Track recipe lineage across shares
- Share permissions: Control read-only access and re-sharing

PRE-TEST SETUP
You'll need 2 iOS devices for full testing:
- Both devices with TestFlight installed
- Both signed into iCloud (Settings > Apple ID)
- Both with Heirloom installed from TestFlight
- Internet connection on both devices

QUICK TEST FLOW (10 minutes)

Device A (Sender):
1. Open Heirloom and navigate to any recipe
2. Tap the Share button (top right)
3. Fill out the share form:
   - Message: "Testing CloudKit!"
   - Your name: [Your name]
   - Permission: Read-Only
   - Expiration: 7 days
4. Tap "Create Share Link"
5. Wait for CloudKit URL to generate
6. Send link via Messages to Device B

Device B (Receiver):
1. Open Messages and tap the CloudKit share URL
2. Review the recipe preview sheet
3. Check that sharer name and message appear
4. Tap "Add to My Recipes"
5. Verify recipe appears in your recipe list
6. Open recipe and confirm all content is there
7. Check for "Shared by [Name]" provenance badge

WHAT TO TEST

Core Functionality:
- Can create shares without errors
- Share URL generates and sends
- Share preview loads on receiving device
- Recipe imports with all content intact
- Provenance shows correctly ("Shared by...")
- Generation tracking works (Generation 1, 2, etc.)

Error Handling:
- Try creating share with Airplane Mode on
- Try accepting share with Airplane Mode on
- Decline a share (should close cleanly)

Performance:
- Share creation completes in under 5 seconds
- Share preview loads in under 3 seconds
- No app crashes or freezes

Re-sharing (Advanced):
- On Device B, re-share the imported recipe
- Accept on another device or back to Device A
- Verify generation increments (Gen 1 to Gen 2)

Priority Levels:
- P0: App crashes, data loss, can't use core features
- P1: Major features broken or not working as expected

CLOUDKIT DASHBOARD (Optional)

For advanced testing, you can monitor CloudKit records:
1. Go to: https://icloud.developer.apple.com/dashboard
2. Select "Heirloom" app
3. Select "Development" environment
4. Navigate to Data > Private Database
5. Look for CKShare records created during your test

SUCCESS CRITERIA

You've successfully tested the build if:
- You can share a recipe from Device A
- Device B receives and accepts the share
- Recipe appears in Device B's collection
- All recipe content preserved (title, ingredients, instructions)
- Provenance metadata shows ("Shared by [Name]")
- No crashes or critical errors

QUESTIONS OR ISSUES?

If you encounter critical bugs or have questions, use TestFlight's built-in feedback tool or email with screenshots/screen recordings when possible.
