WHAT TO TEST (BETA NOTES)

Welcome to Heirloom! Thank you for helping test our recipe management app.

What's New in This Build:
- Recipe import from web URLs (AllRecipes, Serious Eats, King Arthur, and more)
- Smart shopping list with iOS Reminders integration
- Step-by-step cooking mode with progress tracking
- Dynamic recipe scaling (adjust serving sizes on the fly)
- Advanced search and filtering by source, favorites, and cooking history
- iCloud sync across all your devices

Key Features to Test:

1. Recipe Import
   - Tap the + button and choose "Import from URL"
   - Try pasting recipe links from AllRecipes.com or SeriousEats.com
   - Check that images, ingredients, and instructions import correctly

2. Shopping List
   - Add recipes to your shopping list
   - See how ingredients are automatically combined
   - Export to iOS Reminders (requires permission)
   - Check off items as you shop

3. Cooking Mode
   - Open any recipe and tap "Start Cooking"
   - Navigate through steps with large, readable text
   - Mark steps as complete
   - Notice how "times cooked" increments when you finish

4. Recipe Scaling
   - Adjust servings with +/- buttons on recipe detail view
   - Watch ingredient quantities scale automatically
   - Try different multipliers (0.5x to 10x)

5. Filters & Search
   - Use the filter button to narrow recipes
   - Sort by date added, title, or times cooked
   - Search by recipe name

What to Look For:
- Any crashes or freezes
- UI elements that feel confusing or hard to use
- Import failures from recipe websites
- iCloud sync issues between devices
- Shopping list math errors when combining ingredients
- Anything that doesn't feel quite right

Known Issues:
- Some recipe websites may not import correctly (we're adding more sites)
- First-time iCloud sync can take a few moments

Send Feedback:
Use the Settings tab → "Send Feedback" to report bugs or suggest features.

🐛 Bug Fixes in 1.0.1:
  - Fixed critical issue where ingredient fractions (1/2, 1/4, etc.) weren't being parsed correctly
  - Fixed grocery category auto-assignment (ice cream, frozen items, beverages now categorized correctly)
  - Added support for unicode fractions (½, ¼, ⅓, ¾)

  ✅ Quality Improvements:
  - Added comprehensive test suite (93 tests, 94.6% passing)
  - Improved parsing for ingredient ranges (e.g., "1/4-1/2 tsp")
  - Enhanced ingredient categorization accuracy

FULL DESCRIPTION (FOR APP STORE)
================================================================================

Recipes Worth Passing Down

Heirloom is a beautiful, modern recipe app designed to preserve the dishes that matter most. Whether you're saving Grandma's secret cookie recipe, importing a weeknight dinner from your favorite food blog, or building your own cookbook from scratch, Heirloom makes it simple and delightful.

IMPORT FROM ANYWHERE
Paste a URL from AllRecipes, Serious Eats, King Arthur Baking, or any site with structured recipe data. Heirloom automatically extracts ingredients, instructions, cooking times, and photos.

SMART SHOPPING LISTS
Add recipes to your shopping list and watch Heirloom intelligently combine ingredients. Need 2 cups of flour for cookies and 3 cups for bread? Heirloom shows you need 5 cups total. Export directly to iOS Reminders to check off items while shopping.

COOK WITH CONFIDENCE
Enter Cooking Mode for a distraction-free, step-by-step experience. Large text makes it easy to read from across the kitchen. Mark steps complete as you go, and Heirloom tracks how many times you've made each dish.

SCALE ON THE FLY
Feeding a crowd? Halve or double any recipe instantly. Heirloom recalculates all ingredient quantities and displays them with proper fractions (½, ¾, etc.).

FIND RECIPES FAST
Filter by source (family recipes, imported, manual), favorites, cooking history, or what's already in your shopping list. Sort by date, title, or how often you make each dish.

SYNC ACROSS DEVICES
Your recipes sync automatically via iCloud. Add a recipe on your iPad while browsing, then cook from it on your iPhone in the kitchen.

DESIGNED FOR HOME COOKS
- Warm, nostalgic design inspired by classic cookbooks
- Intuitive interface that feels natural from day one
- No ads, no subscriptions, no gimmicks
- Built with SwiftUI for iOS 17+ with privacy in mind

PERFECT FOR:
- Preserving family recipes before they're lost
- Building a personal cookbook of favorites
- Planning weekly meals and grocery trips
- Scaling recipes for dinner parties or meal prep
- Keeping all your recipes in one organized place

Heirloom respects your privacy. Your recipes are stored locally on your device and synced via your personal iCloud account. No third parties can access your data.

PRIVACY POLICY SUMMARY
================================================================================

Data Collection:
- We use Mixpanel for anonymous analytics (feature usage, crash reports)
- No personal information is collected
- All recipe data is stored locally and in your personal iCloud account

Data Sharing:
- Recipe data is never shared with third parties
- iCloud sync uses your Apple ID (end-to-end encrypted)
- Analytics data is aggregated and anonymized

Your Control:
- Delete all data anytime from Settings
- Disable iCloud sync in iOS Settings → iCloud
- Recipe data is yours forever
